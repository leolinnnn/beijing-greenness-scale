# Beijing research record

[← Research and code](../README.md)

**Zihao Lin.** *A Road-Segment Comparison of Street-View GVI and NDVI:
Scale-Dependent Associations and Classification Differences within Beijing's
Fifth Ring Road*. Research manuscript; this repository does not assert journal acceptance.

The README reproduces Figures 9 and 7 from the manuscript dated **21 October 2026**.
The PNGs retain the original Chinese labels, intervals and map credits, with English
captions in the README. They are unchanged from the previously shared portfolio figures.

The statistical workflow starts from the corrected fixed paired-road measures.
Its sampling specifications and code history are recorded in [PROVENANCE.md](../PROVENANCE.md).
Beijing GVI combines vegetation and terrain; street-view dates span 2013–2023 and
the Landsat scene is from 7 September 2021. Category differences measure sensitivity
to the measurement scale within a fixed road sample.
